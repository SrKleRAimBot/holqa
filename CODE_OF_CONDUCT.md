# Code of Conduct

Holqa adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. All contributors and maintainers are expected to follow it in every project space, including
issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels.

## Our standards

We expect participants to be respectful and welcoming, to give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and to focus
on what is best for the community. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing others' private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. Until a dedicated conduct contact is published, contact the
repository owner through their GitHub profile. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
